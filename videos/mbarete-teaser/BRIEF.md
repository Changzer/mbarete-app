## Intent

A 12–15s high-impact teaser for Mbarete, the bilingual (EN/中文) sourcing app:
Apple/Coca-Cola/Adidas-grade motion graphics with the real app in demo. Story
the user asked for: first the app running on a computer screen, then a zoom
out to a phone in hands taking a picture of a product at a market, the AI
reading the handwritten price board and cataloguing it. Focus on usability —
the camera-to-catalog speed is the message. Product truth: photo capture +
AI transcription into a bilingual product catalog is the hero; orders
secondary; finance not shown.
